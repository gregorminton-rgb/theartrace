rights to them and releases them under the terms below.

## Music (`music/`)

All tracks are AI-generated by the project author.

| File | Played |
| --- | --- |
| `trading-cities.mp3` | standing in a port city, or ashore |
| `Gallery Walk.mp3` | the auction room |
| `Horizon's Lullaby.mp3` | ocean voyages (alternates with the next entry) |
| `Open Sea Reflections.mp3` | ocean voyages (alternates with the previous entry) |
| `Quiet Uplift.mp3` | voyages by train |

Licence for all music files: CC BY 4.0. Attribution: "TheArtRace by
gregorminton-rgb".

`ship.mp3` and `travel.mp3` are earlier drafts that the game no longer
plays. They are kept in the repository but are not loaded.

## Images

- `images/` (city and scene pictures) and `images/art/` (artwork pictures)
  were created with AI image tools by the author and processed with the
  scripts in `tools/`.
- `images/scenes/` contains auction-room plates derived from the same images.
- Painting titles and artist names refer to historical works and are used
  descriptively.

Images: CC BY 4.0. Attribution: "TheArtRace by gregorminton-rgb".
